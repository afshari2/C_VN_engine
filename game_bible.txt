# Game Bible: A Cat in Girls’ Dormitory

**Genre:** Adult Ecchi / NSFW Dating Sim Visual Novel (Cat Protagonist)  
**Engine:** Custom C + SDL2 VN Engine (PC-98 retro aesthetic)  
**Target Audience:** 18+  
**Version:** 1.0 (Cleaned & Optimized)

## Core Fantasy

You play as **Tama** (customizable name), a stray cat adopted into **Sakura Heights**, an old, charming all-girls dormitory.  
Through cat-like actions, exploration, and adapting to each girl’s personality, you build deep affection and increasingly explicit relationships.

The game heavily emphasizes **room-based exploration**, personality-driven gameplay, and voyeuristic cat POV.

## Story Opening (Prologue)

On a rainy night, a soaked stray cat takes shelter in front of Sakura Heights dormitory.  
The next morning, Himari finds the cat, brings him inside, cleans and dries him.

**Tutorial Interaction (Cat POV):**
- The player (as cat) scratches Himari while she tries to pet him.
- She puts the cat on her lap anyway, gives him the name **Tama**, and explains the rules.
- She teaches the core gameplay loop:  
  > “Every girl is different. Watch them carefully, understand their personality, and act accordingly to gain their affection.”

This scene serves as the **game tutorial**.

## Gameplay Systems

### Time System
Each day is divided into **5 phases**:
- Morning
- Noon
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Night

### Movement & Rooms
- From the central corridor/lounge, the player chooses which room to visit.
- Rooms can be single or double occupancy.
- The game is designed for easy expansion — new rooms and girls can be added modularly.

### Affection System
- Each girl has her own **Affection** value (0–120).
- Affection is displayed on the right side of the screen (Name + Room Number + Heart Bar).
- Actions give + or – points depending on how well they match the girl’s personality.

### Night Restrictions
- **≥20 Affection**: Allowed to enter room at night (sleep, cuddle, watch changing, etc.).
- **≥40 Affection**: Can stay for more explicit night scenes (masturbation watching/participation).

### Lewd Progression (NSFW)
- **50+ Affection**: Can join shower scenes.
- **70+ Affection**: Can stay during masturbation scenes.
- **100+ Affection**: The girl offers a **collar**. Accepting it locks you into her route and triggers her **personal ending**.

**Important:** All scenes should be written and illustrated as suggestive, ecchi, or fully NSFW depending on context. Character sprites must include **underwear and nude variants**.

### Special Story Elements
- Some storylines involve the girl being on her **period** or **ovulating** (higher lewd potential).
- Trans-feminine characters are supported in single rooms.

## Content Scope (Realistic & Sustainable)

**Per Girl:**
- 80-100 unique story segments total
- 15-20 segments per time of day (Morning/Noon/Afternoon/Evening/Night)
- 4–5 dedicated Ending scenes

This provides excellent variety while remaining achievable.

Technical Requirements

Auto-detection system for characters and rooms.
Strong debug logging (console output for loaded scripts, missing files, variable changes, etc.).
MVC architecture friendly.
Modular script loading per room + time of day.
NO multiple sprite rendering, always only one character showing on screen and talking.



Tone & Style

Playful, mischievous, shamelessly ecchi.
Heavy use of cat POV angles (low ground, looking up).
Retro PC-98 visual style with retro lewd artwork.
All interactions are cat actions only (no spoken dialogue from the player).



Expansion Philosophy
The entire game is built for easy modular expansion:

Adding a new girl = create folder + files + add to rooms.json
Game automatically detects and integrates the new character.
